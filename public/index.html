The hard question

What if someone takes the pot and stops paying?

Every member puts down a small deposit. When you receive the pot, part of what you still owe is held back and released as you keep paying. Trust lowers the holdback.

Slot 1, 5 members, ₦5,000 eachHoldback by trust

Most of the pot reaches you right away. A good record means even less is held back.

Deposit up front

Every member puts down one payment before the circle starts. It covers a missed payment before their turn and comes back at the end.

Holdback from the payout

When you receive the pot, a percentage of what you still owe is held back and released each round as you keep paying.

Settled by the rules

If someone stops paying, anyone in the circle can run settlement. The holdback pays first, then the deposit, then a shared default pool.

Trust lowers the cost

A clean record lowers your holdback and a default raises it. Vouching, where members back each other, is planned.

Honest limit: with a small holdback, a new member who stops paying can leave a gap the default pool may not fully cover, so a receiver could get a little less. Invite-only circles, visible records and vouching keep that risk low.

Nobody runs off with the pot

Test money only. A full review comes before any real money is used.

No organizer withdraw

There is no function that lets the circle's creator take money out. Only the payout rules move it.

Rules fixed at start

Amount, group size and payout order are set when the circle starts. Nobody can change them after members join.

A clear schedule

The first payout runs as soon as everyone has paid. Every round then gets a fresh countdown that restarts after each payout.

Receipts for everything

Every payment and payout creates a receipt in your portfolio and in your email.

A small fee you can see

A 1% fee is taken from each payout, and the receiver sees it before the circle starts.

Private by design

Circles are invite only, so people save with others they know.

Roadmap

How it scales

Anyone with an account can start a circle. Every circle has its own rules and record, so thousands can run side by side.

Today

  • Private, invite-only circles of 3 to 10 people
  • Payments in naira through Paga
  • Starts only when every seat is filled
  • Automatic payout into your portfolio
  • Transfer to your bank in one tap
  • Deposit and holdback protect every circle

Next

  • Larger circles and flexible schedules
  • Vouching, so members can back each other
  • Standing orders so payments run by themselves
  • Verified bank names before every transfer

Later

  • Public circles people can find and join
  • Other currencies and countries
  • Rewards for finished circles
  • Mobile app and easy invite sharing
